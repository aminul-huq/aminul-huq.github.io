Bio

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Nevada, Reno, working on robust and generalizable deep learning for heterogeneous visual domains.

My research is centered on domain adaptation and computer vision, with applications in medical imaging and precision agriculture. I am particularly interested in how models can preserve task-relevant information while adapting to differences introduced by imaging devices, acquisition environments, sensors, and other domain-specific factors.

Across my current work, I study both training-time adaptation for data augmentation and test-time generalization across domains. My research also includes evaluating adaptation quality through downstream tasks and feature-space distribution measures.

Current Focus

Medical imaging: cross-device mammography adaptation, preservation of anatomical and diagnostic information, and robust downstream classification.

Precision agriculture: UAV-based detection and quantification of crop abnormalities, selective domain adaptation, and generalization across field conditions.
