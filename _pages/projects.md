## Mammography Domain Adaptation

A domain adaptation framework for reducing acquisition-device shift in mammograms. The work studies adaptation between mammography devices while evaluating both distribution alignment and preservation of clinically meaningful information.

**Topics:** CUT, CycleGAN, diffusion-based adaptation, MMD, CORAL, feature alignment, mammography classification.

## UAV Maize Abnormality Detection

Domain adaptation for abnormality identification in UAV crop imagery. The project investigates selective adaptation and data augmentation to improve generalization across agricultural fields.

**Topics:** UAV imagery, domain adaptation, Vision Transformers, crop abnormality detection, selective augmentation.

## Robust Deep Learning

Earlier work includes adversarial learning, image classification, and robustness under distribution or input perturbations.
