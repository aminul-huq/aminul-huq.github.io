My research centers on **robust and generalizable deep learning under heterogeneous data distributions**. I am particularly interested in settings where models must generalize across different acquisition devices, environments, or institutions.

## Medical Image Analysis

My current work studies domain shift in mammography, with emphasis on cross-device adaptation. I investigate methods based on image-to-image translation, representation alignment, and downstream validation to determine whether adaptation improves distribution alignment while preserving clinically relevant structure.

Key themes include:

- Mammography device adaptation
- Anatomical and semantic preservation
- Distribution alignment in feature space
- Cross-device downstream evaluation
- GAN- and diffusion-based adaptation

## Precision Agriculture

I also study domain shift in UAV imagery for crop monitoring. This work examines how domain adaptation and selective augmentation can improve abnormality detection when models are transferred across fields or acquisition conditions.

## Broader Direction

My broader goal is to develop learning systems that remain reliable when the deployment distribution differs from the training distribution, especially in high-impact visual applications.
