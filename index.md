## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the **University of Nevada, Reno**, working in computer vision and deep learning. My research focuses on learning under distribution shift, particularly **domain adaptation**, **medical image analysis**, and robust visual learning.

A central theme of my work is to make deep learning systems more reliable when training and test data come from different domains. I study this problem in applications such as **mammography** and **UAV-based precision agriculture**, where changes in imaging devices, acquisition conditions, or environments can substantially affect model performance.

### Research Interests

- Domain Adaptation and Domain Generalization
- Medical Image Analysis and Mammography
- Deep Learning and Computer Vision
- Generative Models for Image Translation
- Representation Learning and Robustness
- Precision Agriculture

## Current Research

### Domain Adaptation for Mammography
I study device-level distribution shifts in mammography, including adaptation between images acquired using different mammography systems. The goal is to improve cross-device generalization while preserving clinically important anatomical information.

### Domain Adaptation for Precision Agriculture
My work in UAV imagery investigates domain adaptation and selective data augmentation for identifying abnormal maize plants across fields and acquisition conditions.
